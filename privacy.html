Summary

The Values Auction House ("the App") is designed around one principle: your reflections stay on your device. By default, we collect nothing, transmit nothing, and operate no account system. The App makes no background network requests and integrates no advertising SDKs, analytics, or fingerprinting libraries.

This page explains that in detail, plus the disclaimers that matter for a self-reflection exercise.

Privacy

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device by default. Specifically, the App does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • Your bids, holdings, journal entries, or reflections.
  • Identifiers such as your Apple ID, advertising ID (IDFA), device fingerprint, or IP address.
  • Crash logs tied to your identity. Anonymous diagnostics may be enabled by you via iOS Settings.

Where your data lives

  • On-device only. Your portfolio and reflections are written to the App's local sandboxed storage and to the encrypted iOS backup of your device.
  • iCloud Backup (optional). If you have iCloud Backup turned on for this device, a copy of that local data may be included in your encrypted iCloud backup. It remains encrypted at rest and is never accessible to us.
  • Reset wipes everything. Tapping Reset Portfolio in Settings returns the App to a clean slate, including all locally stored portfolio data. Uninstalling the App removes its local storage entirely.

Photos & camera

  • If you choose an avatar or save a shareable report, the image is written to your local Photos library only when you explicitly tap Save.
  • The App never accesses the camera or photo library in the background, and never reads photos you did not select yourself.

System APIs we use

The App uses a small set of system APIs for purely local purposes. These are disclosed in our app bundle's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest:

  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults — to remember your settings (theme, last opened lot, etc.). Reason code CA92.1.
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryFileTimestamp — to record when you acquired each holding. Reason code C617.1.
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategorySystemBootTime — to schedule local notifications correctly across reboots. Reason code 35F9.1.

None of these calls transmit data off-device.

Third parties

  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or attribution SDKs are integrated.
  • No social-network logins, no sign-in with Apple, no email account is required.
  • The only network calls in the entire App are initiated by you from the in-app Support view, to open a mail or web link you chose.

Children

The App is rated 4+ and contains no targeted advertising or third-party tracking. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). Because we collect nothing at all, this requirement is satisfied by design.

If a future version adds cloud sync

If we ever introduce optional cloud sync, account features, or sharing, we will:

  • Ask for your explicit, in-app consent before any data leaves your device.
  • Update this page and the App Store listing before the feature ships.
  • Give you a one-tap way to disable the feature and delete any synced copy.

Permissions

iOS may prompt you for the following permissions, only when you take the corresponding action:

  • Photo Library (write) — to save a shareable report image when you choose Save Report to Photos.
  • Notifications — if you opt in to gentle reminders to revisit your reflection. You can decline and the App works fully without them.

You can review and revoke any granted permission anytime in iOS Settings → VFSAFETY.
