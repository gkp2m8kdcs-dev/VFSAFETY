Frequently asked questions

How do I back up my portfolio?

Your portfolio lives in the App's local storage on your iPhone or iPad. If you have iCloud Backup or iTunes/Finder syncing turned on for this device, your data is included in those encrypted backups automatically. There is no separate cloud copy we control. See Your data & backups below for the full picture.

How do I move my portfolio to a new device?

Restore the new device from your most recent iCloud Backup (or encrypted Finder/iTunes backup) of the old device. The portfolio is part of the App's local data and will reappear after restore. If you only need a single portfolio, use Settings → Export Report in the App to save a PDF you can keep as a personal record before you wipe the old device.

How do I reset everything?

Open Settings → Reset Portfolio in the App and confirm. This deletes all holdings, journal entries, reflections, and saved drafts. Uninstalled the App? iOS will also purge the local storage.

Why can't I save my report to Photos?

The App uses the standard iOS Photo Library Add permission. If you previously selected "Don't Allow", iOS won't ask again automatically. Go to iOS Settings → VFSAFETY → Photos → Add Photos Only and try saving again.

Notifications aren't arriving. What should I check?

Three places: (1) iOS Settings → VFSAFETY → Notifications — make sure they're enabled and the style is set to banners; (2) In the App, Settings → Reminders — confirm the time and cadence you chose; (3) Check Focus modes — a focused state may be silencing the App.

Does the App work offline?

Yes — entirely. The App makes no network calls of its own. Any link you tap from the Support view (to email us, for example) requires internet only because your mail app does.

Will my portfolio sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Not in the current version. Each device has its own independent portfolio. A future version may offer optional iCloud sync with explicit consent — see Privacy.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email support@vfsafety.app or use Settings → Send Feedback in the App. Please include your iOS version, device model, and — for bugs — steps to reproduce.

Troubleshooting

The App crashes on launch

  • Force-quit and reopen: swipe up from the app switcher and relaunch.
  • Reboot the device.
  • Make sure iOS is up to date (Settings → General → Software Update).
  • If the problem continues, email support@vfsafety.app with your iOS version and device model.

Something in the chart looks wrong

Charts are computed live from your local data. Try Pull to refresh on the dashboard. If the issue persists, resetting and rebuilding the portfolio is the cleanest way to rule out a stale state — export a report first if you want to keep a copy.

I lost data after an iOS update

iOS updates occasionally clear app data if the device was low on storage during the update. Restore from your most recent iCloud or encrypted computer backup. If neither is available, we're sorry — we do not retain a server-side copy because we do not operate one.

Your data & backups

The short version:

  • Where it lives: On your device, in the App's sandboxed storage.
  • What's included in iCloud Backup: Everything local, encrypted.
  • What we see: Nothing. We do not operate a server.
  • How to wipe it: Settings → Reset Portfolio, or uninstall the App.
