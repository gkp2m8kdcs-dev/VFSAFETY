What it is

A quiet auction house
for the things you cannot buy back.

Not a productivity tracker. Not a personality quiz. A reflective space that uses the metaphor of an auction to surface what you're really investing your years in — Character, Bonds, Vitality, Autonomy, Prosperity, Stature.

Character

Integrity, Faith — the things that stay valuable when no one is watching.

Bonds

Family, Love, Service — the people whose names you carry into old age.

Vitality

Health, Adventure — the energy to actually live the life you bid on.

Autonomy

Freedom, Creativity, Wisdom — authorship over your own hours.

Prosperity

Wealth, Security — comfort that lets you say yes to the right things.

Stature

Influence, Recognition — the quiet weight of a life well-shaped.

How it works

Four rounds.
One portfolio of a life.

A short, focused run that takes most people ten to fifteen minutes. You can repeat it whenever the season changes.

01

Open the catalogue

Fourteen lots across six categories — each with a base price that reflects how easily it tends to be lost.

02

Bid your stake

Allocate a share of your attention, time, and capital. Outbid yourself honestly — that's the whole point.

03

Reflect

A guided round of prompts helps you name what each win actually means for the year ahead.

04

Hold & revisit

Your portfolio sits on-device. The What-If simulator lets you replay the auction with different bids.

Common questions

Before you bid.

Is this a clinical or psychological assessment?

No. The Values Auction House is a self-reflection exercise. The reflections, insights, and chart summaries are educational prompts — not a diagnosis, treatment plan, or substitute for professional care.

Do you collect my data?

No. Your bids, holdings, journal entries, and reflections are stored locally on this iPhone or iPad. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not operate an account system.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free to download with no ads. Any paid tier would be clearly disclosed before purchase.

Can I reset everything?

Yes. Settings → Reset Portfolio returns the app to a clean slate, including all locally stored portfolio data.
